# COS-2 — Hair & Scalp Science, Dryness, Damage & Conditioning

**Estimated learning time:** 120 minutes

## Purpose
Connect hair/scalp science, visible assessment, cosmetic pH concepts, conditioning selection, and damage prevention to safe cosmetology service decisions.

## 1. Hair Structure and Condition
The visible hair shaft includes the cuticle and cortex, with a medulla present in some hair. Service planning can be influenced by texture, density, porosity, elasticity, prior chemical/heat exposure, tangling, and breakage.

### Hair Observation
☐ Texture
☐ Density
☐ Porosity clues
☐ Elasticity considerations
☐ Dryness
☐ Roughness
☐ Tangling
☐ Breakage
☐ Previous chemical/heat exposure

## 2. Visible Scalp Observation
Under clean, consistent lighting, observe visible dryness, oiliness, flakes, buildup, redness, abrasions, unusual lesions, or other changes. Ask about comfort, sensitivities, reactions, and recent services.

**Do not diagnose visible findings as eczema, psoriasis, infection, alopecia, or another medical condition.**

## 3. Dry Scalp and Cosmetic Product Selection
Visible flaking or a dry-feeling scalp can have many causes. Cosmetic observation alone cannot determine the cause. Within scope, a practitioner may consider gentler labeled cleansing/conditioning products, reduced irritating heat/friction, and client comfort. Persistent, severe, painful, inflamed, bleeding, or otherwise concerning findings should be appropriately referred.

## 4. Damaged Hair and Conditioning
Rinse-out conditioners can improve manageability and slip. Leave-in products may provide additional lubrication/protection. Strengthening/protein-containing products may be appropriate when the product directions and hair condition support their use. More product is not automatically better.

## 5. Cosmetic pH Education
pH describes how acidic or alkaline a water-based solution is. Product formulation and pH can influence hair behavior, especially during chemical services. Use manufacturer specifications or appropriate measurement when pH information is needed.

**A photograph alone does not provide an exact scalp pH measurement. CrownPath AI must never invent one.**

# COS-4 — Chemical Services & Product Safety

**Estimated learning time:** 150 minutes

## Purpose
Build disciplined chemical-service safety habits for consultation, manufacturer instructions, preliminary testing, product handling, ventilation, PPE, timing, monitoring, emergency response, cleanup, and documentation.

## READ — CONSULT — TEST — CONTROL — MONITOR — STOP — DOCUMENT

### READ
Read complete instructions for the exact product system before mixing or applying.

**Product/system:** ____________________________________________

**Mixing ratio from manufacturer:** _____________________________

**Processing limit:** __________________________________________

**PPE:** ____________________ **Ventilation:** _________________

### CONSULT
Confirm requested service, relevant prior chemical services, sensitivities, previous reactions, visible condition, and product history.

### TEST
Perform preliminary, patch, allergy-alert, or strand testing whenever required by manufacturer instructions or applicable rules. Do not invent a universal testing method.

**Required test:** _____________________________________________

**Directions reviewed:** ☐ Yes

### CONTROL
Use clean compatible containers/applicators, accurate measurement, required PPE, ventilation, client protection, correct application sequence, and timing.

### MONITOR
Monitor comfort and visible response. More time or stronger chemistry is not automatically better.

### STOP
For significant burning, breathing difficulty, swelling, severe discomfort, or another concerning reaction, stop and follow the exact product immediate-response instructions. Seek appropriate emergency/professional care when indicated. Do not diagnose the reaction.

### DOCUMENT
Record required products, formulation details permitted by workflow, timing, tests, observations, unexpected events, and client-care instructions.

> Never improvise chemical mixtures or exceed manufacturer directions to chase a result.

# COS-5 — Non-Surgical Hair Replacement

**Estimated learning time:** 180 minutes

## Purpose
Teach cosmetic hair-replacement fundamentals: consultation, system selection, sanitation, visible scalp/hair observation, fitting, lawful attachment concepts, blending, maintenance, removal, documentation, and referral boundaries.

## Cosmetic vs. Surgical
CrownPath may teach wigs, toppers, partial systems, integration pieces, and other lawful non-surgical cosmetic systems.

CrownPath does **not** teach:
- incision;
- graft harvesting/extraction;
- graft placement;
- implantation into living tissue;
- anesthesia;
- surgical hair transplantation;
- post-surgical medical management.
