# CrownPath — Future Hybrid Electric Smart Classroom

**Status:** Future concept / deferred. Not implemented or approved for release.

## Owner-approved direction

Build toward a combined physical and digital learning environment that
incorporates all of the following:

- Electrically powered cosmetology and barber training stations, safe
  equipment operation, and instructor-led practical education.
- Virtual and hybrid classrooms with interactive lessons, textbooks,
  remote instruction, student avatars, and AI teaching assistants.
- Educational scalp and hair imaging using magnification cameras,
  informed consent and privacy protections. No claim of medical X-ray
  imaging, diagnosis, or medical-device capability.
- Bot Camp for beginner-to-advanced AI, digital literacy, simulations,
  programming, and potentially robotics education.
- School-scoped instructor and learner permissions, equipment consent,
  auditable access, and student progress tracking.
- Optional future Bluetooth and Wi-Fi connectivity subject to real
  browser/hardware compatibility and security validation.

## Implementation sequencing

1. **Now:** Finish the existing isolated, test-only CrownPath authorization,
   school scope, and device visibility security checks.
2. **Later:** Draft curriculum, user journeys, equipment requirements,
   accessibility, privacy impact assessment, and safe demonstration designs.
3. **After explicit review:** Build simulated classroom workflows and
   instructor-approved prototypes; do not connect real hardware by default.
4. **Separate owner decision:** Evaluate equipment procurement, training
   facilities, regulations, costs, and any live release.

The existing 30-day program may include a prototype plan; it is not a
commitment to construct a fully equipped physical classroom in 30 days.

## Boundaries

This roadmap entry is not a feature flag, production deployment, live
payment flow, hardware integration, patent claim, or assertion of being
first in the world. No merge or production changes without explicit approval.
