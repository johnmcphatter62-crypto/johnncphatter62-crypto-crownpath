# CrownPath Scalp Care Foundations pilot worksheet

Participant code: __________   Pathway: Barber / Cosmetology

## Practice consultation

Read Hair & Scalp Science in your assigned pathway. The cases below are fictional. Record visible details separately from what the client reports. Use the exercise to practice communication and deciding whether to continue a cosmetic service.

**Case A.** Before a planned wash and style, you notice fine white flakes on the hair near the part. The client reports that their scalp sometimes feels tight after washing. They report no discomfort today.

- What do you see? What did the client report?
- What two questions would you ask before deciding whether to proceed?
- Would you continue or modify the planned service? Explain your choice using the information available.
- Write one sentence you could say to the client that describes your observation without giving it a medical label.

**Case B.** Before a planned chemical service, the client says the scalp feels painful. You notice an area of broken skin where the product would be applied.

- What do you see? What did the client report?
- What would you do about the planned service?
- Write a brief response that explains your decision respectfully.

## Facilitator review

Review the responses before recording completion. In Case A, look for a clear distinction between what the learner saw and what the client said, neutral questions, and a reasoned service decision. In Case B, the learner should stop the planned chemical service and explain the decision without attempting diagnosis. Ask the learner to revise any answer that misses that stop decision before marking the safety check complete.

Record the participant code, lesson completion, exercise completion, safety check result, and feedback in the pilot results record. Use fictional cases during the dry run and leave client names and images out of the worksheet.
