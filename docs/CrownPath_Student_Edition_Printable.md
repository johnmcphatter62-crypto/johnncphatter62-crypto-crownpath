# CrownPath Student Edition
## Printable Learning Workbook

**Edition:** Student / Learner-Safe

This workbook is designed to accompany CrownPath digital lessons, instructor demonstrations, guided practice, and practical checkoffs. It intentionally excludes instructor answer keys, rationales, instructor notes, private records, and restricted operational information.

> **Important:** CrownPath educational material does not by itself create professional licensure, medical authority, massage licensure, State Board credit, or permission to perform regulated services. Current official requirements, lawful scope, instructor direction, employer procedures, and manufacturer instructions control where applicable.

---

# How to Use This Workbook

For each lesson:
1. Read the lesson purpose and learning objectives.

3. Observe the instructor or approved digital demonstration.
4. Complete guided practice.
5. Answer the knowledge-check questions without using an answer key.
6. Complete the practical checkoff with an authorized instructor where required.

# PART I — HOME CARE

## HC-1 — Client Safety & Home Care Foundations

### Purpose
Build safe, respectful habits for non-medical home-care support while protecting dignity, choice, privacy, and professional boundaries.

### Learning Objectives
- Identify basic environmental and client-safety priorities.
- Protect dignity, privacy, choice, and personal boundaries.
- Explain non-medical role limits.
- Use a structured task routine.
- Recognize when concerns require escalation.

### Student Service Sequence
1. Check the environment for obvious hazards before beginning a task.
2. Explain what you are about to do and obtain the client's cooperation.
3. Use clean hands and appropriate protective practices.
4. Document or report concerns according to approved procedures.

### Guided Practice
- Identify hazards in a simulated room.
- Practice explaining a task while preserving client choice.
- Sort example requests into within-role, supervisor/escalation, and emergency categories.
- Practice factual observation language without diagnosis.

## BAR-5 — Client Consultation & Shop Safety

**LOOK:** visible condition without diagnosis.

**INSPECT:** tools, blades, guards, cords, surfaces, client protection.

**MONITOR:** heat, pressure, comfort, unexpected reaction.

**STOP:** follow approved procedure when safety requires it.

**RESET:** clean, disinfect, store, dispose, document.
